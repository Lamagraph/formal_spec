LamaGraph formal_spec

A formal specification, developed in Rocq, of an interaction-nets-based co-processor (accelerator) architecture: the abstract interaction-net model, the concrete architecture, and the equivalence between them.

The documentation below is extracted from the .v sources, which are the specification itself.
